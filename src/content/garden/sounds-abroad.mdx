[Sounds Abroad](https://soundsabroad.app) is a 3D globe for finding trending music by country. Claude Code wrote most of the code. This note is about what it couldn't do.

<Image src="/garden/sounds-abroad.png" alt="Sounds Abroad with South Korea selected on the globe and its chart below" width={240} height={519} className="my-5 rounded-lg border border-underline" />

## How I worked

I wrote a PRD first. Then I cut scope until only the core was left. I worked in thin vertical slices, one small PR at a time. A second agent and CodeRabbit reviewed the changes. What to accept stayed my call.

## What the AI got wrong

The AI was sure the chart was server-rendered. Types, lint, tests, and build all passed. A controlled A/B showed it wasn't. The sheet sat inside a Radix Portal, which doesn't SSR.

Deferring the globe raised the LCP score. But no one got a better experience. So I refused it and redefined the gate around FCP, CLS, and TBT.

## The gesture I replaced

V2 shipped on June 25. A flick spun the globe and settled on a country. The goal was to make small countries easier to pick. It didn't work. Neighbouring countries were still a few pixels apart under a fingertip.

In July I wrote a new decision. You drag, and the country at the centre is highlighted. You release, and you land there. It's only partly shipped so far.
