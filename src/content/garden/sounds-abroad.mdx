[Sounds Abroad](https://soundsabroad.app) is a 3D globe for finding trending music by country. I built it mostly with Claude Code. The longer version is on the [project page](/sounds-abroad).

<Image src="/garden/sounds-abroad.png" alt="Sounds Abroad with South Korea selected on the globe and its chart below" width={240} height={519} className="my-5 rounded-lg border border-underline" />

## How I worked

I wrote a PRD first. I worked in thin vertical slices, one small PR at a time. A second agent and CodeRabbit reviewed the changes.

## The gesture I replaced

V2 shipped on June 25. A flick spun the globe and settled on a country. The goal was to make small countries easier to pick. It didn't work. Neighbouring countries were still a few pixels apart under a fingertip.

In July I chose a new gesture. You drag, and the country at the centre is highlighted. You release, and you land there. It's partly shipped.
