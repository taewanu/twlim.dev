Welcome to the garden of curiosity. I believe genuine curiosity and love are the two pillars for a world full of what matters.

Nothing starts as a fully grown tree. It starts as a small seed. People are the same, and so are ideas.

If we were already perfect, where would the fun be? Isn't this journey on earth about having fun along the way?

Oh baby, baby, it's a wild world. But who knows? As Jim Carrey once said, "It can be rough out there, but ... there's soft serve ice cream with sprinkles."

Let's learn and grow together.

## Stages

Every note shows its stage.

- **Seed:** rough and early
- **Sapling:** cleaned up
- **Mallorn:** reasonably complete, still tended

The names come from Samwise Gamgee, who planted a silver nut at Hobbiton that grew into a mallorn.

## Dates

Planted is when I started a note. Tended is the last meaningful edit.

## Changes and corrections

Notes can change without notice, and [git history](https://github.com/taewanu/twlim.dev/commits/main/) keeps the record. Corrections are welcome by [email](mailto:twlim.dev@gmail.com).
